Plain English Guide

How Your Desk Companion Works

The complete 5-step loop from hearing your voice to moving its head.

🎙️

Step 1: Hearing & Perception (The Ears)

A tiny microphone mounted in the bot's head quietly listens for a wake phrase (like "Hey companion"). It barely uses any battery or computer power until it hears you call it.

👂

Step 2: Turning Voice Into Words

Once it hears you speak, an offline hearing program instantly turns your voice into written words, taking less than half a second.

🧠

Step 3: Thinking & Remembering (The Brain)

The text is sent to your AI brain program (like Ollama) running locally on your computer. An added memory layer reminds the bot what you like, what you talked about yesterday, and what personality it should have.

🗣️

Step 4: Speaking Out Loud (The Voice)

The brain's written reply turns into human-sounding speech and streams straight out of the deskbot's speaker without any awkward pauses.

🤖

Step 5: Physical Movement & Expressions

While speaking, the robot nods or tilts its head using tiny quiet motors, blinks animated eyes on its circular screen, and changes colored LED lights to match its mood.
