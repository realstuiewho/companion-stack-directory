🤖 Beginner Deskbot Roadmap

Step 1 (The Brain): Run Ollama or LM Studio on your home PC or Mac.

Step 2 (The Ears & Voice): Connect an INMP441 microphone and MAX98357A speaker amp to an ESP32 microcontroller.

Step 3 (Motion): Hook up 2 micro servos using an inexpensive pan-tilt bracket to make your robot nod and turn.
